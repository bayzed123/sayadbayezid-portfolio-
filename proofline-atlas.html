Arif Gadgets — a complete shop for customers, staff, and the owner.

A mobile-first commerce system that takes orders, resolves volume pricing, supports bKash/Nagad/Rocket/bank transfer/COD, tracks courier delivery, protects stock and margin calculations, and gives staff a Bangla operating handbook.
