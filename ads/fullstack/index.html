The design map

How it actually works, start to finish

Six stages. You are involved in three of them and free during the other three. Nothing is invoiced before the scope is agreed in writing.

  1. Brief and scope

    A 20-minute call: what you sell, who buys it, how they pay, who will run the dashboard. It ends with a written scope and a fixed price — no open-ended hourly billing.

    You + me · day 1

  2. Design system first

    Colour tokens, type scale, spacing, one signature interaction — decided before any screen is laid out. That is why new pages stay consistent later without re-briefing anyone.

    Me · days 2–4

  3. Build on the edge

    Storefront, dashboard and API in one TypeScript repository on Cloudflare. One type describes a product, and all three import it — so a change cannot silently break one of them.

    Me · the main stretch

  4. Tracking and courier wired

    Pixel plus Conversions API with shared event IDs, courier API for booking, payment instructions, order tracking. Tested with Meta's own Test Events tool before launch, not after.

    Me · before handover

  5. Your review, on real data

    You place a real order, your staff ring up a real walk-in sale, and we fix what feels wrong while it is cheap to fix. Revisions in this stage are included.

    You + me · launch week

  6. Handover in your name

    Domain, Cloudflare, repository and every account transferred to you, plus the Bangla staff guide. You are never locked out of your own shop, and you are not locked to me.

    Yours · from then on

Why the order matters. Tracking is stage four, not an afterthought. Most shops bolt a Pixel on after launch, discover the events are wrong two ad campaigns later, and have already paid for the data they cannot trust. Here the events are defined while the checkout is being built, which is the only time it is free to get right.

The four questions I always get

How long does a full build take?

Three to six weeks for a standard catalogue, depending on how fast product photos and cost prices come back from your side. That dependency is named in the scope, so nobody is surprised by it halfway through.

What does it cost to run every month?

At the volume of a shop like the one in the screenshots above, effectively nothing — everything sits inside Cloudflare's free tiers. The real bill is the domain, roughly ৳1,200 a year. If you outgrow the free tiers, that is a good problem and I will tell you before it happens.

Can my staff actually use the dashboard?

That is what the Bangla guide is for, and why staff roles exist — a salesperson sees orders and stock, not your profit margins. The dashboard in the screenshot is run daily by people who had never used an admin panel before this one.

What if I want to leave later?

Then you leave with everything. The code is in your repository, the domain and Cloudflare account are in your name, and the stack is standard TypeScript that any competent developer can pick up. There is no proprietary layer holding you here.
