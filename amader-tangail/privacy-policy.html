Privacy Policy — Amader Tangail (আমাদের টাঙ্গাইল)

Last updated: [July 24, 2026]  |  Developer: Sayad Bayezid & Team  |  Contact: support@sayadbayezid.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Amader Tangail ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the rights you have over it. It applies whether you use the Android app, the installable web app (PWA), or browse as a guest without an account.

1. Summary (plain-language version)

  • You can browse the emergency directory, blood donor list, heritage guide, and news without creating an account.
  • Creating an account (Google Sign-In) is only required to register as a blood donor, post an urgent blood request, or post a classified ad.
  • We collect the minimum needed to run these features: name, phone number, blood group, general location (upazila/union), and whatever you choose to put in a listing or request.
  • We use Firebase (a Google service) to store and secure this data. We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can request correction or deletion of your data at any time — see our Account & Data Deletion page.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

FeatureData collected
Blood donor registrationName, phone number, blood group, upazila/union, donation history you enter
Urgent blood requestPatient name, condition, hospital/location, blood group, bags needed, contact phone
Classifieds (Haat-Bazar)Ad title, category, price, description, upazila/area, your name, contact phone, photos
News tipsWhatever content you submit
Contact/feedback formName, email or phone, and your message

2.2 Information from Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo (if public) via Firebase Authentication. We never receive your Google password.

2.3 Information collected automatically

Usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics): app opens, screens viewed, general interactions, device/app info. Aggregated, not used to individually identify you for marketing. We do not collect precise GPS location — the upazila selector is a manual filter, not device tracking.

3. How We Use Information

To operate donor listings, connect blood requests to nearby donors, publish classifieds, verify phone numbers via OTP/SMS, respond to support requests, maintain app performance, and prevent abuse or fraudulent listings. We do not use your data for automated decisions with legal effects on you.

4. Guest Access vs. Signed-In Access

Searching, browsing, and calling a donor does not require an account. An account is required only to create content — because that content includes your contact information shown to other users, requiring a verified way to prevent impersonation.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 What's shown publicly, on purpose

This is a directory app — donor profiles, urgent blood requests, and classified listings are visible to anyone browsing that section, including guests, by design. If you'd rather not share your phone number publicly, don't register as a donor or post a listing.

5.2 Service providers

  • Firebase / Google Cloud — our backend (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • SMS/OTP provider — used solely to verify phone numbers at registration.
Section 5.3 (Advertising): applies only once AdSense/AdMob is enabled. If ads are live, Google may use the advertising ID and device info to serve and measure ads; opt out via device settings or adssettings.google.com. Remove this note and add the full section back once ads are actually enabled, matching your Play Console Data Safety declaration.

5.4 What we never do

We do not sell your personal data, and do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing without your consent.

5.5 Legal disclosure

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or valid request from Bangladeshi authorities, or to protect user safety.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and listing data while your account is active or your listing is posted. Request deletion any time via our deletion page.

7. Data Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all data, Firebase Authentication for identity, Firestore Security Rules restricting read/write access, and OTP verification for phone numbers. No system is 100% secure, but we use reasonable, industry-standard measures.

8. Your Rights

Access, correct, or delete your data, or withdraw Google Sign-In consent (via your Google Account settings) at any time by emailing support@sayadbayezid.com or using our deletion page.

9. Children's Privacy

Amader Tangail is intended for a general audience and not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Content & Age Appropriateness

Amader Tangail contains no violence, sexual content, gambling, or promotion of controlled substances or age-restricted goods. The app does include user-to-user contact features (calling donors, contacting ad posters).

11. Your Rights Under Bangladeshi Law

Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 establishes rights over personal data, including consent requirements (enforcement mechanisms phase in through 2027). We aim to align our practices with this framework. Users outside Bangladesh may have additional rights (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) — contact us for applicable requests.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
