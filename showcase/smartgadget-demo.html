SmartGadget

A wholesale electronics shop, and the dashboard its owner runs it from.

Open the live demo→ Ask for this, rebranded
  • Storefront
  • Admin dashboard
  • Wholesale pricing
  • GA4 + Meta Pixel

A complete wholesale electronics storefront for the Bangladeshi market — catalogue, tiered wholesale pricing, product pages, cart, checkout and order tracking — plus the admin dashboard the owner runs it from: products, stock, orders, customers, reviews, content and analytics.

Who it is for. Importers and wholesalers who sell the same item at different prices depending on quantity, and need one screen that shows what is in stock and what is owed.

Core features

What it does

Tiered wholesale pricing

The price changes with the quantity, and the server decides it — the browser cannot claim a price it has not earned.

Catalogue to paid order

Browse, product page, cart, checkout and order tracking all work end to end. Nothing in the flow is a dead link.

The admin the owner lives in

Products, stock, orders, customers, reviews, site content and analytics — the screens a shop is actually run from, not a KPI wall.

Tracking that reports once

GA4 and the Meta Pixel share one event ID, so a sale is counted a single time instead of twice.

Opens with no sign-in

The dashboard is unlocked on purpose so you can look straight through it. Every product, order, customer and figure in it is generated.

Why choose this

What you get that a template does not give you

Every demo is the real build

Nothing here is a mockup or a design file. Each one is deployed, and you click through it the way a customer would. If a link is dead you will find it, which is the point.

You get the admin, not just the shop

Most storefront demos stop at the shop front. The part that decides whether a business can actually run on it is the dashboard behind it, so that is included and unlocked.

Invented data, never borrowed

Every product, order, customer and figure in these demos is generated. No real client's catalogue, orders or customers appear in any of them.

Rebrandable before it is rebuilt

Colours, type and logo are variables, not decisions baked into markup. The first change you ask for should not need a rewrite.

Built for Bangladeshi connections

Tested at phone width on a slow connection, because that is how most of the traffic arrives — not on a desk at 1440px.

One person, start to finish

The same person writes the front end, the API and the deploy pipeline. There is no handover gap for a bug to live in.
