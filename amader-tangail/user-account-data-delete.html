How long it takes

A person reviews every request before anything is removed, because deletion is permanent and a mistake cannot be undone. Requests are completed within 30 days, and normally far sooner. The status page above tells you the moment it is done.

Retention after deletion

We do not keep a copy of deleted personal data beyond what's required for fraud prevention or legal compliance, which we retain for up to 90 days before permanent removal.
