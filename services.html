Design · from $70

UI/UX that's built as a system, not a one-off

Who it's for

Founders and teams whose site or product works but doesn't look like it's worth what they charge — or agencies who need design work handed off in a state their own developers can actually build from.

What's included

A real design system first — color tokens, type scale, spacing, a signature interaction — then screens built from it, not the other way around. This exact page was built with that process: palette and type decided before a single section was laid out.

How it's delivered

Static HTML/CSS/JS or Figma files, whichever your team can actually use. No proprietary tools you'll be locked out of later.

Why results hold up

A documented system means new pages stay consistent without you re-briefing a designer every time — the design decisions are written down, not memorized.

Development · from $150

Full-stack builds on infrastructure that doesn't fall over

Who it's for

Anyone who needs an actual working product — a dashboard, an internal tool, a client-facing SaaS — not a static brochure site with a contact form bolted on.

What's included

Front-end build, backend API, database schema, and the CI/CD pipeline so every push deploys itself — the same setup running SmartLeadGen and this site's own backend today.

How it's delivered

Cloudflare Workers + D1 by default — serverless, globally distributed, no server to patch or reboot at 3am. GitHub for version control and CI/CD, matching how SmartGen and SmartLeadGen are both already run.

Why results hold up

Cloudflare's edge network means the same build performs well whether your users are in Dhaka or Dallas — no separate "we'll add a CDN later" conversation.

Marketing · from $100

SEO, tracking, and lead generation that's built, not just "set up"

Who it's for

Businesses who know they need SEO and ad tracking but have had it "set up" by three different freelancers, none of whom left documentation.

What's included

Technical/on-page SEO, Meta Pixel + Conversions API with proper event deduplication, GA4, and — where it fits — active lead sourcing using our own tooling, not a manual spreadsheet.

How it's delivered

For lead generation specifically: SmartLeadGen, a tool built in-house, searches Google's Places API for businesses matching your target profile and scores each one by how weak their online presence already is — so outreach starts with the easiest prospects to convert, not a cold list.

Why results hold up

Dual-delivery tracking (browser Pixel + server-side Conversions API) means your ad platform sees conversions that ad-blockers and Safari's tracking prevention would otherwise hide — you're optimizing on real numbers, not a partial picture.

FAQ

Before you reach out

How long until I see SEO results?

Usually 8–10 weeks for early movement, depending on your domain's history and how competitive your keywords are. Genuinely competitive terms take longer — I'll give you an honest estimate for your specific site before we start, not a generic promise.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of what you're trying to fix or launch, and access to whatever's relevant (site login, ad account, etc.) once we've agreed on scope.

Do you work with fixed pricing or hourly?

Fixed pricing per project by default, using the starting prices above as a baseline — you'll get an exact quote before anything begins, no surprise invoices.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes — the "Fullstack customization" tier exists exactly for that: design, development, and marketing infrastructure as one coordinated build instead of three separate hand-offs.

What makes the Marketing service different from a typical SEO freelancer?

Access to tooling built in-house rather than rented — SmartLeadGen for lead sourcing, and the same Pixel/CAPI setup running on this site, not a generic checklist copied between clients.

Not sure which one you need?

That's a normal first conversation — tell me what's not working and I'll tell you honestly where to start.
