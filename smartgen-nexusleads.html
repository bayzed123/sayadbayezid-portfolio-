A deliberate pipeline

Every step has a reason.

The product is designed for agencies and operators who want repeatable research rather than a one-off scrape. The platform keeps credentials, usage, and workspace state separated from the public marketing layer.

Push the approved workspace to CSV or Google Sheets and use the outreach draft as a starting point, not a substitute for judgment.

Built for a careful workflow.

When future tools are ready, their real links can be added to the same page without changing the product story.

Is NexusLeads a scraped contact dump?

No. The intended flow is discovery, review, verification, enrichment, and export. Operators decide which records are useful before outreach.

Can I use my own provider credentials?

The documentation describes a BYOK model so provider credentials can remain under the operator’s control rather than being hard-coded into the public page.

Where will future tools appear?

Future tool links can be added as cards in the feature grid and tracked as ViewContent interactions without creating separate product pages.
