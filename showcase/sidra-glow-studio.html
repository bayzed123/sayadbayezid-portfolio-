The customer side of Sidra Glow Studio, a skincare shop and beauty-treatment studio in Tangail: filter by skin type and concern, ingredient lists and best-before dates on every product, a skin quiz that builds a routine within your budget, routine sets, and facials bookable online against a free slot — with the bookings diary and the order pipeline behind them in one admin.

Who it is for. Salons and studios that also sell product, and currently run the diary and the shop as two separate things.

Core features

What it does

Bookings and orders in one place

The diary — confirm, move, done, no-show — sits in the same dashboard as the product orders. One business, one screen, instead of a paper diary beside a shop admin.

Book a facial against a real free slot

The customer picks from slots that are actually free, so a booking is not a request somebody has to phone back about.

A skin quiz that respects a budget

Answer the quiz and it builds a routine within what you said you would spend. It recommends without overselling, which is what makes it get used.

Ingredients and best-before, shown

Every product carries its ingredient list and its date. In skincare both are things customers check before buying.

Batches picked first-expiry-first-out

Stock is tracked by batch, and the earliest-expiring is packed first — so the dates on the shelf stay honest.

Why choose this

What you get that a template does not give you

Every demo is the real build

Nothing here is a mockup or a design file. Each one is deployed, and you click through it the way a customer would. If a link is dead you will find it, which is the point.

You get the admin, not just the shop

Most storefront demos stop at the shop front. The part that decides whether a business can actually run on it is the dashboard behind it, so that is included and unlocked.

Invented data, never borrowed

Every product, order, customer and figure in these demos is generated. No real client's catalogue, orders or customers appear in any of them.

Rebrandable before it is rebuilt

Colours, type and logo are variables, not decisions baked into markup. The first change you ask for should not need a rewrite.

Built for Bangladeshi connections

Tested at phone width on a slow connection, because that is how most of the traffic arrives — not on a desk at 1440px.

One person, start to finish

The same person writes the front end, the API and the deploy pipeline. There is no handover gap for a bug to live in.
