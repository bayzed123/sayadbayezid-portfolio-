Before you send

Questions I get every week

What exactly do I get for $100?

A working shop: your catalogue, product pages, a bag, a checkout that takes cash-on-delivery orders, and order tracking by number — built mobile-first with the Meta Pixel wired in. It is the shop, not the back office. The dashboard with revenue and profit is the next package up, and you can move up to it later without rebuilding what you already paid for.

Can you build exactly what I have in mind, not one of these packages?

Yes — that is the normal case, not the exception. The packages are starting points so you have a number in your head before we speak. Describe what you need in the form and the scope gets shaped around your requirement, with the price agreed in writing before anything starts.

How long does it take?

A Starter shop is usually one to two weeks. A complete build is three to six, depending mostly on how fast product photos and cost prices come back from your side. That dependency is named in the scope, so it is not a surprise halfway through.

What does it cost to run every month?

At the volume of a shop like the ones in the demos, effectively nothing — it sits inside Cloudflare's free tiers. The real bill is your domain, roughly ৳1,200 a year. If you outgrow the free tiers that is a good problem, and I will tell you before it happens rather than after.

Can my staff actually use the dashboard?

That is what the Bangla guide is for, and why staff roles exist — a salesperson sees orders and stock, not your profit margins. The dashboards in the demos are run daily by people who had never used an admin panel before.

Will my Facebook ads actually be able to track sales?

Yes, and this is the part most builds get wrong. The Pixel alone loses a real share of mobile traffic to ad-blockers and iPhone privacy settings. Every build here also sends events server-side through the Conversions API with a shared event ID, so Meta counts each sale once and still sees the ones the browser copy lost.

What if I want to leave later?

Then you leave with everything. The code is in your repository, the domain and hosting are in your name, and the stack is standard TypeScript any competent developer can pick up. There is no proprietary layer holding you here.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. The defaults on this page — cash on delivery, local couriers, a Bangla staff guide — are what Bangladeshi shops need. If you are elsewhere, those are swapped for what your market uses. Say where you are in the form.
