A complete fashion retail front end: homepage, category listing with live filters and a price slider, product page with gallery and reviews, cart, and a three-step checkout with shipping and payment. Built on Bootstrap 5, so your colours, type and logo go in without rewriting anything.

Who it is for. Businesses that already have a commerce backend — or a developer who will build one — and need the shop front without a rewrite.

Core features

What it does

Fourteen screens

Home, category, product, cart, a three-step checkout, login, register, password reset, and four admin screens.

Live filters and a price slider

Filtering happens as you move the slider, with no page reload.

Three-step checkout

Details, shipping and payment as separate steps — the shape most customers already recognise.

Rebrandable, not rewritable

Bootstrap 5 means your colours, type and logo are variables. Nothing needs restructuring to look like your business.

Front end only, deliberately

It drops onto whatever backend you already use, or we wire one up. Said plainly here so nobody buys it expecting a database.

Why choose this

What you get that a template does not give you

Every demo is the real build

Nothing here is a mockup or a design file. Each one is deployed, and you click through it the way a customer would. If a link is dead you will find it, which is the point.

You get the admin, not just the shop

Most storefront demos stop at the shop front. The part that decides whether a business can actually run on it is the dashboard behind it, so that is included and unlocked.

Invented data, never borrowed

Every product, order, customer and figure in these demos is generated. No real client's catalogue, orders or customers appear in any of them.

Rebrandable before it is rebuilt

Colours, type and logo are variables, not decisions baked into markup. The first change you ask for should not need a rewrite.

Built for Bangladeshi connections

Tested at phone width on a slow connection, because that is how most of the traffic arrives — not on a desk at 1440px.

One person, start to finish

The same person writes the front end, the API and the deploy pipeline. There is no handover gap for a bug to live in.
