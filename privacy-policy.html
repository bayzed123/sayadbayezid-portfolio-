1.Who We Are

Connect with Bayezid is a digital agency based in Bangladesh. www.sayadbayezid.com is our website and personal portfolio, hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. We also operate other products, including SmartGen (smartgentools.com) and applications built on Meta's developer platform (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp Business Platform) — each of those has its own detailed policy, linked in Section 13.

2.Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

When you use our contact form, email us, or message us, we receive whatever you choose to share — typically your name, email address, and the content of your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our analytics providers automatically collect basic technical information when you visit: IP address, approximate location, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring pages.

2.3 Information from Meta Platform integrations

If a page or feature connects to Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, or WhatsApp, the data practices for that specific integration are covered in full in our Meta Platform Products Privacy Policy, not repeated here.

3.How We Use Information

  • Respond to your messages and inquiries
  • Operate, maintain, and secure our website
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it
  • Comply with legal obligations

4.Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may limit some features.

5.How We Share Information

We share information only with the service providers who help us run this site (such as our hosting and analytics providers, under confidentiality obligations), when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal data.

6.Data Retention

We keep contact messages and related data only as long as needed to respond to you and for our own reasonable business records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

7.Data Security

Our site is served over HTTPS/TLS, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8.Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, or Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 — including the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

9.Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the higher age set by local law) without appropriate consent.

10.Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites, including our own other properties and Meta's platforms. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate.

11.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We'll revise the "Last updated" date above, and post material changes prominently on our website.
