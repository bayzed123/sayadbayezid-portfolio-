2.Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

Depending on which product you use, this may include your name, email address, phone number, business name, job listings or applications, catalog/product information, payment or order details (processed by Meta or a licensed payment processor, not stored by us in raw form), messages you send us, and any information you submit through a contact form, signup flow, or WhatsApp conversation.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit our website or use our tools, we and our service providers may automatically collect device and log information: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, timestamps, and approximate location derived from your IP address. See Section 8 for how cookies and similar technologies are used.

3.WhatsApp Business Platform — How We Handle Your Data

Because messaging carries especially sensitive information, we're giving WhatsApp its own detailed section covering every WhatsApp Business Platform product we use.

Cloud API & Cloud API — Calling

We send and receive WhatsApp messages (text, media, template messages, and interactive replies) and, where enabled, voice/video calls through Meta's Cloud API. Messages travel end-to-end encrypted between you and Meta's servers; Meta decrypts them to route to our systems, where we can read the content to respond to you. Call metadata (initiation, duration, and status) is handled the same way when Calling is active.

WhatsApp Business Account Management

We manage business profile details tied to a WhatsApp Business Account — display name, category, profile photo, business description, and the verified phone number(s) connected to that account.

Embedded Signup

When a business connects its WhatsApp number to our systems through Meta's hosted Embedded Signup flow, we receive the business's WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, and the permissions granted during that flow — not your Facebook password or unrelated account data.

WhatsApp Flows

Where we use WhatsApp Flows (structured in-chat forms — for example, booking an appointment or completing a short survey), we receive the responses you submit inside that form.

Marketing Messages API & Direct Send API

We use pre-approved message templates to send opt-in marketing, reminder, or notification messages, and we track delivery, read, and opt-out status so we can honor your preferences.

Coexistence — Messaging

Where a business uses the WhatsApp Business app and the Cloud API on the same number at the same time, conversations may be visible and synced across both interfaces for that business's own team.

Opt-in, opt-out, and how we use WhatsApp messaging

  • We only message you on WhatsApp after you've shared your number and clearly opted in to hear from the specific business (Connect with Bayezid or the client business we're messaging on behalf of).
  • We use WhatsApp messaging strictly for the purpose disclosed at opt-in — for example, customer support, order updates, appointment reminders, or the marketing communications you agreed to receive. We do not repurpose WhatsApp conversation data for unrelated advertising.
  • Where we use automation or bots inside WhatsApp, they perform clearly defined, predictable business tasks (like routing a support request or confirming a booking) rather than open-ended, general-purpose AI chat, consistent with Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" (or the equivalent local keyword), blocking the business number, or contacting us directly — see Section 17.
  • We do not ask for or store full payment card numbers, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, or health information through WhatsApp.
  • On Meta's Cloud API infrastructure, message content and per-message user identifiers are retained by Meta for a maximum of 30 days to support core delivery features (like retransmission); our own retention of conversation history on our side is described in Section 9.

5.Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases: your consent (for example, WhatsApp opt-in or marketing emails, which you can withdraw at any time), performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing our services (balanced against your rights), and compliance with a legal obligation. Hashing personal data before sending it to Meta for advertising (see Section 2.3) does not by itself provide a legal basis — where we build Custom Audiences from customer data, we first confirm we have valid consent or another lawful basis for that use.

6.How We Share Information

  • With Meta: by the nature of these integrations, relevant data flows to and from Meta Platforms, Inc., which processes it under its own Platform Terms and Privacy Policy.
  • With client businesses: where Connect with Bayezid builds or manages a Meta integration (Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp number, or ad account) on behalf of a client, information you send to that integration is shared with that client business, which is responsible for its own use of your data.
  • Service providers: hosting, analytics, email, and payment providers who process data on our behalf, under confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Legal & safety: where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Connect with Bayezid, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy's protections continuing to apply.

We do not sell personal data.

7.Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website and tools may use cookies, pixels, and local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure traffic, and, where enabled, support Meta's App Events / Conversions API for ad measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may limit some features.

8.Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations, or to resolve disputes. When a client relationship or app connection ends, or Meta's own Platform Data rules require it, we delete or de-identify the associated Platform Data unless we're legally required to keep it. WhatsApp conversation history is retained only as long as needed to support the customer-service purpose it was collected for, and no longer than our contractual arrangement with the relevant business.

9.Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction — including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and hashing of identifiers before they're shared with advertising platforms. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10.International Data Transfers

We are based in Bangladesh, and Meta's global infrastructure may process and store data outside your own country. Where required, we and Meta rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses or an equivalent transfer mechanism) for these cross-border transfers.

11.Children's Privacy

Our products are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age required by applicable law (generally 13, or higher where local law sets a higher age) without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12.Your Privacy Rights

If the GDPR/UK GDPR applies to you

You have the right to access, correct, or erase your data; restrict or object to processing; request data portability; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting past processing.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, correct inaccurate information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal data). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Under Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act, 2026

As a data fiduciary/controller under this Act, we recognize your rights to access, correct, and erase your personal data, and to object to automated decision-making, consistent with the Act's consent-centric framework.

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact details in Section 17 or the deletion steps below.

13.Data Deletion Instructions

This section doubles as our Data Deletion Instructions for Meta Platform apps — the same URL you're on now can be used as both the Privacy Policy URL and the Data Deletion Instructions URL in any Connect with Bayezid app's dashboard.

  1. To remove an app's access to your Facebook or Instagram account: open Facebook, go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, find the relevant Connect with Bayezid (or client) app, and select Remove.
  2. To stop WhatsApp messages: reply STOP to any conversation, or block the business number directly in WhatsApp.
  3. To request full deletion of any data we hold about you: email us at the address in Section 17 with the subject line "Data Deletion Request," including the product/app name and the email, phone number, or account you used.
  4. We will confirm your request and complete deletion — except data we're legally required to retain — within 30 days.
