Privacy Policy — Amader Tangail (আমাদের টাঙ্গাইল)

Last updated: 20 September 2026  |  Developer: Sayad Bayezid & Team  |  Contact: support@sayadbayezid.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Amader Tangail ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the rights you have over it. It applies to the Android app and to the same app running in a web browser, whether you sign in or browse as a guest.

1. Summary

  • You can browse the emergency directory, the district and union directory, the blood donor list, the heritage guide, the news and the classifieds without creating an account.
  • An account (Google Sign-In) is required only to register as a blood donor, post an urgent blood request, or post a classified ad.
  • Anything you post in those three places — including your phone number — is shown publicly. That is what the app is for.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and the app carries no advertising.
  • The app requests exactly one Android permission: internet access. It does not ask for your location, camera, contacts or storage.
  • You can have your account and data deleted at any time, from the app or from the web without signing in — see Delete Your Account & Data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

A photo attached to a classified ad is stored inside that listing's record, in the same database as the rest of the listing.

2.2 Information from Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo (if your Google profile has one) and a Google account identifier, through Firebase Authentication. We never receive your Google password.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics, through Google Analytics for Firebase: app opens, which screens are viewed, general interaction events, and device and app information such as model, operating system version and app version. This is used to understand how the app is used, not to identify you personally or to market to you.
  • A Firebase installation identifier, which Analytics requires. It identifies an installation of the app, not a person, and it is reset if the app is reinstalled or its data is cleared.
  • Your IP address is visible to the services listed in section 5 whenever the app contacts them, as it is with any internet request.

We do not collect precise or approximate device location. The app has no location permission and no location code. The upazila and union you see are a menu you choose from by hand, not a reading of where your phone is.

3. What We Do Not Collect

Stated plainly, because it is easier to verify than a list of what we do collect. The app contains no advertising SDK, no attribution or marketing SDK, no crash reporting, no social network SDK, and no push notification service. It does not read your contacts, your photo library, your calendar, your microphone, your camera or your files. It declares one Android permission, INTERNET, and nothing else.

4. How We Use Information

To publish donor listings, blood requests and classified ads so other people in Tangail can find and contact you; to reply to messages you send us; to understand which parts of the app are used, in aggregate; and to detect and remove abusive or fraudulent listings. We do not use your data to make automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 What is public, on purpose

This is a directory. Donor listings, urgent blood requests and classified ads — including the phone number attached to them — are visible to anyone using the app, including people who are not signed in. If you would rather your phone number were not public, do not register as a donor and do not post a listing. You can ask us to remove a listing at any time.

Donor details are not verified. The app asks a new donor to confirm a code during registration, but that code is generated on your own device and no message is sent to your phone network. It confirms nothing about who owns the number. Donor names, phone numbers and blood groups are self-reported by community members, and blood group in particular is never checked by us. Always confirm details directly with the donor, and go through a hospital or a recognised blood bank before any transfusion.

5.3 Advertising

The app serves no advertisements. It contains no AdMob, no AdSense, no Meta or Facebook SDK, and no attribution or marketing SDK. No advertising identifier is read, and no data is shared with any advertising network. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated to match.

5.4 What we never do

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing.

5.5 Legal disclosure

We may disclose information where we are required to by law, by court order, or by a valid request from Bangladeshi authorities, or where it is necessary to protect someone's safety.

6. Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and your listings for as long as your account exists and your listings are posted.

To have everything removed, use the form on our Delete Your Account & Data page. It emails you a six-digit code to confirm the address is yours, shows you exactly what is held for it before you decide, and then submits the request. You do not need the app installed and you do not need to sign in.

Each request is reviewed by a person before anything is removed, and completed within 30 days — normally much sooner. When you submit it you are given a reference; entering that reference with your email address on the same page tells you whether the request is still queued, has been carried out, or was not approved and why. If you would rather not use the form, emailing support@sayadbayezid.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" still works and is handled by hand on the same commitment.

After deletion we do not keep a copy of your personal data beyond what is needed for fraud prevention or legal compliance, which is held for up to 90 days and then permanently removed. Aggregated analytics that cannot identify you are not affected.

Full detail, including a table of exactly what is deleted, is on the Delete Your Account & Data page.

7. Data Security

All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS/TLS. Identity is handled by Firebase Authentication, so we never see or store a password. Firestore Security Rules restrict which records an account can read and write, and a listing can only be deleted by the account that created it. No system is completely secure, but these are reasonable, industry-standard measures.

8. Your Rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. You can withdraw Google Sign-In access at any time from your Google Account permissions. Deletion is self-service on the deletion page, which also shows you what is held before you commit to removing it and lets you check the status of a request afterwards. For a copy or a correction, email support@sayadbayezid.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Amader Tangail is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will remove it.

10. Content and Age Appropriateness

The app contains no violence, sexual content, gambling, or promotion of controlled substances or age-restricted goods. It does include user-to-user contact: you can call a donor or an ad poster directly from a listing, and listings are written by other users rather than by us.

11. Your Rights Under Bangladeshi Law

Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 establishes rights over personal data, including requirements around consent, with enforcement mechanisms phasing in through 2027. We aim to align our practices with that framework. If you are outside Bangladesh you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA — contact us and we will handle your request under those rules.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. If a change materially affects what we collect or who we share it with, we will say so in the app.
