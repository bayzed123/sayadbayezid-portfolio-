02

SEO — how it actually works

Ranking a site higher on Google comes down to three things working together: targeted keywords so Google understands what you offer, other sites linking to you so Google trusts you, and genuinely useful content so you rank for more than one term.

Results depend on your site's starting point — domain age, current rankings, and how much (good or bad) SEO work has already been done — plus how competitive your keywords are. Expect early movement in roughly 8–10 weeks, not overnight; genuinely competitive terms can take a few months.

FAQ

Before you reach out

How long until I see SEO results?

Usually 8–10 weeks for early movement, depending on your domain's history and how competitive your keywords are. Genuinely competitive terms take longer — I'll give you an honest estimate for your specific site before we start, not a generic promise.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of what you're trying to fix or launch, and access to whatever's relevant (site login, ad account, etc.) once we've agreed on scope.

Do you work with fixed pricing or hourly?

Fixed pricing per project by default, using the starting prices above as a baseline — you'll get an exact quote before anything begins, no surprise invoices.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes — the "Fullstack customization" tier exists exactly for that: design, development, and marketing infrastructure as one coordinated build instead of three separate hand-offs.

Not sure which one you need?

That's a normal first conversation — tell me what's not working and I'll tell you honestly where to start.
