Delete Your Account & Data — Amader Tangail

App: Amader Tangail (আমাদের টাঙ্গাইল)  |  Developer: Sayad Bayezid & Team

If you'd like your Amader Tangail account and the data associated with it removed, follow the steps below. This applies whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@sayadbayezid.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."
  2. Include the phone number or Google account email you registered with, so we can locate your record.
  3. We will confirm your identity matches the account on file, then delete your account and associated data within 30 days, and reply to confirm once it's done.
