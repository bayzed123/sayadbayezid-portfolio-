The customer side of Zamil Shop BD, a baby and kids shop from Dhaka: shop by age from 0–6 months to 5 years, a gift finder, a gift registry for new parents, ride-on toys with real product photos, a Bangla/English switch, cart and Cash on Delivery checkout with bKash, Nagad and Rocket — and the dashboard where those orders arrive.

Who it is for. Baby and kids sellers who lose money to fake Cash-on-Delivery orders and want them flagged before anything is packed.

Core features

What it does

Fake-order protection, colour-coded

Every order is marked Trusted, New or Verify before packing. On Cash on Delivery that judgement is the difference between a sale and a parcel that comes back.

Abandoned checkouts, listed

Carts that reached checkout and stopped are a list you can call, not a number you cannot act on.

Shop by age, and a gift finder

Browsing by 0–6 months through 5 years is how a parent actually shops, and the gift finder is for the person buying for someone else's child.

A gift registry for new parents

Parents list what they need, family buys from it, and nobody receives three of the same thing.

Safety certificates need a document

A product cannot claim a safety certificate without a file attached. The claim and the proof are one field, so the shop cannot drift into a promise it has not got.

Why choose this

What you get that a template does not give you

Every demo is the real build

Nothing here is a mockup or a design file. Each one is deployed, and you click through it the way a customer would. If a link is dead you will find it, which is the point.

You get the admin, not just the shop

Most storefront demos stop at the shop front. The part that decides whether a business can actually run on it is the dashboard behind it, so that is included and unlocked.

Invented data, never borrowed

Every product, order, customer and figure in these demos is generated. No real client's catalogue, orders or customers appear in any of them.

Rebrandable before it is rebuilt

Colours, type and logo are variables, not decisions baked into markup. The first change you ask for should not need a rewrite.

Built for Bangladeshi connections

Tested at phone width on a slow connection, because that is how most of the traffic arrives — not on a desk at 1440px.

One person, start to finish

The same person writes the front end, the API and the deploy pipeline. There is no handover gap for a bug to live in.
